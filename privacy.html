Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Version 2.0

This is what Bloom keeps on your phone, what leaves it and why, who receives it, and what you can do about it — in plain words.

The short version

  • What you log stays on your phone. There is no account to make.
  • Only what a feature needs leaves your phone: your question when you ask Bloom, a call when you call her, and a copy of her memory if you turn the backup on.
  • No ads. No tracking. Nothing is sold, ever.
  • You can export or delete everything from the app, at any time.
  • Questions? Write to support@bloomdays.family.

Who we are

Bloom is made by Roman Franta, an independent developer in Singapore. In this policy “we” means him. Contact: support@bloomdays.family.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log in every chapter — your periods and signs, your pregnancy, your baby's feeds, sleep and diapers, your milk, moods, symptoms, appointments, photos, your notes — is stored on your phone only. So are the voices Bloom knows (the voice prints never leave the phone), the reading of an ovulation-test photo, and your PIN. We cannot see any of it. It is removed when you delete it, when you use Delete all my data, or when you remove the app.

Anthropic and OpenAI

Ask Bloom's answers and Bloom's memory notes are written by Anthropic's Claude models; calls run on OpenAI's voice models. Both receive your data as a service to Bloom, through their business offerings, not their consumer apps. As their own pages said on November 13, 2026:

  • Anthropic (Commercial Terms, effective June 17, 2025): “Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services.” For API users, Anthropic says it automatically deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, and keeps them longer only where needed to enforce its usage policy or by law (retention, July 1, 2026).
  • OpenAI (your data, API): data sent to the OpenAI API “is not used to train or improve OpenAI models”; abuse-monitoring logs are kept for up to 30 days, unless longer retention is required by law or to prevent harm.

A call goes straight from your phone to OpenAI: your voice is not stored by Bloom. You never have to use Ask Bloom or calls; everything else in Bloom works without them.

The memory backup

The backup is off unless you turn it on (Me → Privacy & data → Back up Bloom's memory). Then a copy of what Bloom remembers — who you are, the people and facts you have told her, past moments and open threads; never your logs, your chats or the dated conversation notes — is kept on Bloom's server under your app ID, so a new phone can still remember you. Turning it off, or tapping Forget everything Bloom has learned, deletes that copy.

Purchases

Pro is bought in the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google take the payment under their own policies (Apple, Google). RevenueCat (privacy policy) receives the purchase and your app ID, so Bloom knows Pro is yours. We never see your card or your store account.

When you write to us

If you write to support@bloomdays.family, or send the form on our support page, we receive your email address and your message. A message sent with the form goes through Bloom's small sender on Cloudflare and is delivered by Resend (privacy policy). Bloom's sender keeps no copy; Resend keeps its delivery records under its own policy. We use it only to answer you, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

How this matches the App Store's privacy labels

Bloom's App Store listing shows these kinds of data. Each is linked to you (through your random app ID), used for app functionality only, and not used to track you:

  • Name — your first name and the names of people you tell Bloom about, in the note sent with a question or call.
  • Health — your logs and symptoms in that note, and in Bloom's memory.
  • Sensitive Info — pregnancy or childbirth information, such as your week of pregnancy.
  • Audio Data — your voice during a call.
  • Other User Content — your questions, conversations and Bloom's memory notes.
  • User ID — the random app ID.
  • Purchase History — that you bought Pro.

No ads. No tracking. No selling or sharing for advertising. No analytics or crash reporting in the app.

Your choices

  • Export everything: Me → Privacy & data → Export my data.
  • Delete everything on your phone: Delete all my data.
  • Forget what Bloom has learned, on your phone and in the backup: Forget everything Bloom has learned.
  • Forget a voice, or all of them: Voices Bloom knows.
  • Turn the memory backup off, or never use Ask Bloom or calls.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete anything kept on Bloom's server (your backup, allowance or invites): write to support@bloomdays.family. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Everything that leaves your phone travels over HTTPS. On your phone, your data is protected by your phone's own lock and storage, and Bloom can add a PIN. The memory backup on Bloom's server is protected by Cloudflare's systems and limited access, but it is not encrypted so that only you can read it. No system is perfect; if something ever went wrong with your data, we would tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Consumer health data

Some US state laws, such as Washington's My Health My Data Act, ask apps to say plainly what consumer health data they collect. Bloom collects only what the table above describes. The consumer health data in it is: your chapter, your week of pregnancy and due date; your symptoms and today's logs; while trying, your temperature, ovulation test results, fluid, the days you logged sex and your fertile window; the medicines you track; whether you have had a pregnancy loss; your baby's age and feeds; your voice during a call; your questions and conversations; and the memory backup if you turn it on. We use it only to give you answers, calls and Bloom's memory. We share it only with Anthropic and OpenAI to make those, and with Cloudflare to run Bloom's server. We never sell it. To see or delete it, use the choices above or write to us.

Children

Bloom is made for adults and is not directed to children. It is not for anyone under 16. If we learn that a child under 16 has sent us data, we delete it.

Your rights, wherever you live

We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act. Wherever you live, you can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to take it with you.

  • In the EU and the UK you can also object to how we use your data, stop at any time (stop using the feature, or ask us to delete), and complain to your data protection authority. We use your data only to provide the features you choose to use.
  • In California and other US states you can know, delete and correct your data. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Changes

Each version of this policy is dated. If a change matters to how your data is handled, Bloom will tell you in the app before it applies.
