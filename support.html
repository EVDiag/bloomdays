Common questions

Is Bloom free?

Yes. Bloom is free to use. Pro is one payment in the store, for the full week-by-week journey, the kick counter and the contraction timer. There is no subscription.

Where is my data?

On your phone. What you log stays there. Only what a feature needs leaves it: your question when you ask Bloom, a call's audio when you call her, and a copy of what she remembers if you turn the memory backup on. What leaves your phone.

How do I export or delete my data?

In the app: Me → Privacy & data. Export my data saves everything as a file; Delete all my data removes it from your phone; Forget everything Bloom has learned clears her memory, here and in the backup. For anything kept on Bloom's server, write to us.

Is Bloom a medical device, or birth control?

No, and no. Bloom gives general information and helps you keep track; it does not diagnose or treat. Bloom is not birth control: the fertile window is an estimate and must not be used to prevent a pregnancy. For anything about your health, ask your doctor or midwife. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Which phones does Bloom run on?

iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, and phones with Google Play.

How does Pro work?

You buy Pro once, in the App Store or Google Play, and the store takes the payment — Bloom never sees your card. If you reinstall Bloom and Pro does not come back by itself, write to us with the store's receipt and we will put it right.

What happens when I ask Bloom or call her?

Your question, with a short note of where you are (your chapter, your week or day), goes through Bloom's own server to Anthropic, which writes the answer. A call goes straight from your phone to OpenAI, which runs Bloom's voice. Nothing is used for ads, and Bloom's server keeps no copy of your questions. The details.

How do invites work?

In the app, Us → Invite a friend gives you a code and a link to send. A friend who already has Bloom enters the code in the same place, under Have a code?

Bloom said something wrong — what do I do?

Tell us. Choose Report a bug or Feedback above and write what you asked and what Bloom said. Bloom can be wrong; your doctor or midwife has the last word.
