Five chapters, one companion

Choose where you are, and Bloom shapes itself around it. Move to the next chapter whenever life does.

Cycle

Your period, the days around it, your mood and body. Bloom learns your rhythm from what you log and shows what is coming.

Ask Bloom

Write to Bloom, or call her and talk. She answers in plain words, remembers what you have told her, and says when something needs your doctor or midwife.

  • Questions any hour, in five languages.
  • A voice call when typing is too much — at 3am, with a baby on your arm.
  • She remembers what you share, so you don't start from nothing each time.

To answer, your question goes through Bloom's own server to Anthropic; a call goes straight from your phone to OpenAI. What is sent, and why.

Bring your family in

Bloom is yours, and your people can be part of it.

  • Your circle — the people you choose, in one place.
  • The baby pool — everyone guesses the day and the weight.
  • Letters — words for your baby, sealed until the day you choose.
  • Your book — the journey, kept as a keepsake.

You decide what you share, and with whom.

How it works →

Private by design

  • Your days stay on your phone. There is no account to make.
  • No ads, no tracking, nothing sold — ever.
  • Only what a feature needs leaves your phone: your question when you ask Bloom, a call's audio when you call her, and a copy of what she remembers if you turn the backup on.
  • Export or delete everything from the app: Me → Privacy & data.
